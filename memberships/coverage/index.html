Launch prerequisite: renewal reminders

Renewal reminders are their own feature type, built and shipped before memberships go live for any club. A club can't sell a recurring plan until every row marked Launch prerequisite works end to end.

Regulation

RBI's e-mandate rules require a pre-debit notice at least 24 hours before each recurring charge, and a notice after it. Without them, UPI AutoPay and card mandates can't be used.

Trust

SweatPals sends a notice 24 hours before every renewal. Members who are charged without warning dispute the charge, which costs the club the member and the fee.

Recovery

Failed renewals are only recovered if the member hears about it with a fix-billing link the same day.

What exists today

The backend has scheduled jobs (BullMQ upsertJobScheduler), the outbound email outbox, WhatsApp campaigns and Firebase push. NotificationDelivery is tied to registrations and has one type (ABANDONED_CART), so reminders get their own PlanReminderDelivery model and ClubMembershipSettings (see code/backend/prisma/memberships.prisma).

Club admins configure it for their audience

Nothing about a plan is fixed by peakst8. Each club's admins decide what they sell, to whom, at what price and on what terms. peakst8 only switches the feature on for a club and sets the platform fee. Settings sit at three levels; a lower level never repeats a higher one.

What we couldn't see

  • Exact wording: SweatPals loads its English copy at runtime, so it isn't in the bundles. The prototype uses peakst8 copy.
  • Server rules that never reach the browser: proration maths, retry timing, how ties between two plans are broken. peakst8's rules are written in the note.
  • The SweatPals host dashboard: it's behind login, so host screens come from the help centre only.
  • Mobile app-only flows (guest pass sending, payment methods): described in the help centre, not seen in code.
