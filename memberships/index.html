peakst8 Club Memberships

Pay once a month. Book every run.

Clubs sell plans: a recurring membership or a prepaid pack. Members book each session as they do today, and peakst8 applies the plan at checkout, so the ticket is free, discounted or uses one credit. This note reverse-engineers SweatPals Plans and maps them onto the peakst8 backend.

Launch prerequisite: renewal reminders

Renewal reminders are a separate feature type and ship before memberships go live for any club. A club can't sell a recurring plan until all 12 prerequisite reminder features work.

  • Pre-debit notice at least 24 hours before every recurring charge (RBI e-mandate rule), plus earlier reminders the club chooses.
  • After the charge: receipt on success; payment-failed notice with a fix-billing link on each retry.
  • Before trouble: mandate at risk, and intro offer ending.
  • Rules: club-set channels and send hour; mandatory notices can't be muted; reminders are recomputed on pause, date or price change and cancel; every send is logged.

Full list in the coverage note. Tables: PlanReminderDelivery and ClubMembershipSettings in the schema draft.

In one minute

A plan is not a ticket. It is a pricing rule plus a usage meter, and it plugs into the registration flow peakst8 already has.

1 · HostCreates a plan

Decides what it covers, how often it can be used, the price and billing, and the rules.

2 · HostSets plan price per ticket

On each ticket category, plan holders pay nothing, half, full price or a custom ₹ or % amount.

3 · MemberJoins

Pays, or requests approval and then pays. The plan appears under Passes → Plans.

4 · MemberBooks as usual

Checkout finds the plan, reprices the ticket and records one use. At ₹0, Razorpay is skipped.

5 · SystemRenews or ends

Renews automatically with a notice 24 hours before. If cancelled, access lasts to the end of the period.

How checkout prices a booking

This runs inside the existing proceedToPayment, before the Razorpay order is created.

// input: user U, event E, category C
1  plans  = U's plans (owned or shared) in ACTIVE | CANCELS_AT_PERIOD_END, not expired
2  keep   = coverage includes E (event type or event id), club = E.club or co-host club
3  keep   = has a CategoryPlanPolicy for C
4  keep   = usage meter allows one more booking in the period containing E.startDate
5  price  = FREE 0 · HALF base/2 · FULL base · CUSTOM_PERCENT base×(1−p) · CUSTOM_FLAT max(0, base−x)
6  pick   = lowest price; on a tie, the pack expiring soonest, then a limited plan before unlimited
7  write  = registration.appliedUserPlanId, PlanUsage(HELD)
8  ₹0     → TICKET_GENERATED, no Razorpay order · > ₹0 → order for the reduced amount
9  paid   → usage CONSUMED · failed or expired → RELEASED
10 cancel → RELEASED inside the club's window, otherwise it stays CONSUMED
  • Coupons apply after the plan, and only when the price is still above ₹0. Plans never combine with each other.
  • Credits are counted by event date, so booking next month's run uses next month's allowance.
  • SweatPals has the same step: memberships/eligible-for-bookable-service/:id and payments/public/plan-holders-plans.

Open questions

  • Coverage unit: is a weekly club run one event per session, or one multi-day event? Counting usage by event date assumes one registration per session.
  • Uniqueness: Registration @@unique([eventId, userId]) allows one booking per event. Does that fit how sessions are modelled?
  • Failed renewal: do sessions already booked in the new period stand?
  • Platform fee: the same platformFeePercentage as tickets, or a separate rate for recurring revenue?
  • Refunds: full only, or partial-period too? Who can issue them?
  • Naming: Plans, Memberships or Passes in the app?
  • Pilot: which 2 or 3 clubs, and at what price points?

FAQ

Does buying a plan book me into sessions?
No. Members book each session, and the plan benefit applies at checkout.
Can a member pause their own plan?
No. Only the host can pause, which is also how SweatPals works.
Can plans and coupons stack?
A coupon applies to the price after the plan, if that price is above ₹0. Two plans never combine.
What if a member cancels after a renewal?
There is no automatic refund. Access lasts to the end of the period, and any refund is at the club's discretion.
