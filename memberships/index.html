peakst8 Club Memberships

Pay once a month. Book every run.

Clubs sell monthly memberships and prepaid run packs on peakst8, with perks and brand offers their members can see. Members pay once, keep booking each session as they do today, and the plan applies at checkout. Clubs get predictable monthly income, and their regulars get a reason to stay.

1. The problem

Community fitness clubs on peakst8 run the same sessions every week, but they sell them one ticket at a time.

WhoTodayWhat it costs them
Club adminsEvery weekly run is a separate event with its own ticket. Income depends on how many book that week. Regulars who'd pay monthly either pay per session or pay the organiser directly, outside peakst8. Assumption: off-platform UPI collections are common; confirm in pilot interviews.Unpredictable income and no record of who the paying regulars are. Brand partnerships have nowhere to live, so they can't sell anything.
Regular membersA full checkout every session, at the same price as someone coming once. Nothing shows what being a regular is worth.Friction every week, no reward for loyalty, an easy reason to drift to another club.
peakst8Revenue is a platform fee on each ticket (3% by default). In October 2026 the platform had 244 users and ₹3,85,203 GMV across organisers (source: Organiser and Event Summary, October).GMV moves with each week's bookings. Off-platform memberships cost peakst8 the revenue and the member relationship.

SweatPals, a US community-fitness platform, built exactly this and treats it as core: "Plans create predictable revenue and simplify member access across all experiences" (SweatPals help centre 5.1). We reverse-engineered the whole feature; see the feature list.

2. Who it's for

Primary · club adminsFounders and community leads

Social fitness clubs in Indian metros that run recurring sessions: run clubs (Find Your Fit), women's collectives (Sisters in Sweat), strength and HIIT groups, yoga and pilates collectives. Many already have brand partners.

They need: plans they set up for their own audience, perks and brand offers they control, discounts they can switch on, money in the weekly payout.

Primary · regular membersPeople who come 4 to 12 times a month

They want to show up without paying every time and feel part of the club.

They need: one payment, no surprise charges, and a clear view of what the plan gives them: savings, early access, guest passes, brand perks.

SecondaryStaff, brand partners, peakst8 ops

Approval queues and guest-pass redemption for staff; a perks listing with reveal counts for brands; plan revenue in the existing payout and platform-fee reports for finance.

Not for (yet)Races, network passes, studios

Single tickets already work for one-off races and festivals. Passes across clubs and 1:1 appointments need separate decisions.

Two people to design for (illustrative, based on our pilot clubs):

  • The club founder. Runs a 400-member weekly run club with two volunteers. Has a coffee shop and a running store happy to give members a discount. Wants 100 regulars on a monthly plan to pay pacers and book a track, and a page to point partners and new runners to.
  • The regular. Runs twice a week, paying ₹299 each Saturday and ₹249 each Tuesday. Would pay ₹1,499 a month for no checkout, a free coffee after the long run, and booking the monthly 10K before it sells out.

3. Why now

  • The clubs are already on peakst8 checkout. The Embedded Ticketing pilots take bookings through peakst8, so a plan can apply to bookings they already take.
  • Recurring payments work in India. Razorpay Subscriptions supports UPI AutoPay, cards and eNACH, and peakst8 already uses Razorpay.
  • The backend has the pieces. Scheduled jobs, the email outbox, WhatsApp and push exist; memberships add models and rules, not infrastructure.
  • The model is proven. SweatPals runs it for thousands of communities.
  • Waiting has a cost. Every month a club collects memberships off-platform is a month of members and revenue peakst8 doesn't see.

4. What we're building

1 · Club adminCreates plans

Monthly or quarterly memberships, or run packs: what they cover, how often, price, who can buy, the rules.

2 · Club adminSets plan price per pass

Free, half price, or a custom amount off. That decides what members pay.

3 · MemberJoins

Pays once with a UPI AutoPay mandate, or requests approval first. The plan sits under Passes.

4 · MemberBooks as today

Checkout applies the member price and records one use. At ₹0 there's no payment step.

5 · SystemNo surprise renewals

A reminder before every charge, a receipt after, a fix link if it fails. Cancel is in the app.

6 · MemberSees the value

Savings, early booking, guest passes, the club's brand perks page, and discount codes the club switches on.

Club admins configure all of it. peakst8 only switches memberships on for a club and sets the platform fee. Settings sit at three levels: club, plan and pass. Full list in the feature list.

5. Goals and non-goals

Goals

  1. Clubs earn predictable monthly income on peakst8 from their regulars.
  2. Regulars book with no friction and can see the value of their plan.
  3. No member is ever charged without a reminder first.
  4. Club admins run plans, perks and discounts without help from peakst8.
  5. Clubs show their brand partnerships in one place.

Non-goals

  • Replacing single tickets.
  • Passes across several clubs.
  • Members pausing their own plan (only the club can, as in SweatPals).
  • Migrating memberships from other platforms in V1.
  • A leads pipeline or CRM.

Weekly sessions don't map cleanly to plansMedium · highDecide the session model first (decision 1); usage counts by event date
Clubs over-discountLow · mediumCodes off by default; no stacking with intro offers; usage caps
Brand perks go staleMedium · lowReveal counts and an on/off switch per perk; quarterly review prompt
