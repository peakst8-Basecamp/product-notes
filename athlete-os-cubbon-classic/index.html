What we're building

One peakst8 dashboard where the organiser sells entries on their own site and runs a season of missions, leaderboards and rewards, with no peakst8 engineer in the loop.

  1. Registration on the organiser's site. Embedded Ticketing replaces the KonfHub iframe. Runners register individually or with a club code, and existing KonfHub registrations are imported. See the Embedded Ticketing note.
  2. A programme the organiser builds. Pre-season from registration, then the 8-week Road to Cubbon Classic from 25 Jan 2027, with missions, points and a sponsor slot.
  3. Missions that count themselves. Runs come in from the watch or Strava. Club runs count on a captain's QR scan. Quizzes count when finished.
  4. Leaderboards people care about. Individual, club, age group and gender boards, updating as runs arrive.
  5. Rewards and nudges. Badges, merchandise, partner rewards, free entries and race-day recognition, plus reminders before a streak breaks.
  6. One report. For the organiser, and a weekly summary for the sponsor.

The product screens show nine organiser screens (event home, registration and embed, Road to Cubbon, missions and points, leaderboards, rewards, nudges, clubs, insights) and five runner screens (register on the race site, this week, leaderboard, rewards, quiz). They are new designs in the peakst8 app language. Yoska's screens were used only as a list of capabilities.

Road to Cubbon Classic

A six-to-eight-week digital and physical training programme, "Powered by [Brand]", designed by Nithin Gowda (MyRace). Agreed on 9 Nov: 8 weeks up to race day, live from January 2027 (25 Jan to 21 Mar), after TMM and alongside TCS 10K training.

It includes weekly missions on peakst8, brand-led educational content, product sampling, club engagement, a digital leaderboard, race-day activation and post-race content.

These points are illustrative, not a final scoring system: up to 95 a week, 760 over 8 weeks. Possible rewards: weekly top-performer recognition, Club of the Week, training consistency badges, exclusive Cubbon Classic merchandise, nutrition and recovery partner rewards, special recognition at race-day celebrations, and complimentary entries or partner experiences.

  • KonfHub replaced on cubbonclassic10k.com/register, with every existing registration imported and confirmed.
  • Yoska backend merged into the peakst8 backend: challenges become missions, plus points, leaderboards, levels, quizzes, nudges and activity sync.
  • Yoska UI decoupled: nobody at Cubbon logs into fit.yoska.in.
  • Redesigned Athlete OS dashboard: the organiser configures the programme, missions, leaderboards, rewards and nudges themselves.

  • Nithin onboarded and has set up week 1 of the programme alone.
