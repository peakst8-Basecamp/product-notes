## The problem

Registration runs through a KonfHub widget, so the race loses search traffic and attribution. The engagement programme sits on a coaching tool that wasn't built for events. A runner pays and then hears nothing for four months, and sponsors have no weekly way to reach them.

## Who it's for

- **The organiser:** sells entries on their own site and runs the season themselves.
- **Runners and club captains:** a weekly plan, a leaderboard, rewards worth chasing.
- **Brands:** reach runners every week, with proof.

## Why now

Registration is already open and grows on KonfHub every week, and the organiser has offered to move it. There are 19 weeks to race day: enough to ship in November and measure a full season before the board sees race-day numbers.

## Goals

1. Registration fully on peakst8 by **20 Nov**, with no runner lost in the move.
2. The organiser runs the engagement layer from peakst8 by **30 Nov**.
3. Road to Cubbon Classic runs **25 Jan – 21 Mar**, backed by at least one brand.
4. Show that Athlete OS scales: a 3,500-runner race on one setup, with no engineering for each event.

## Key journeys

- A runner registers on the Cubbon Classic site and pays with peakst8, without leaving the page.
- Runners already on KonfHub get an email that their entry has moved, with a peakst8 ticket.
- A company or club buys in bulk with a group code.
- The organiser sets up each week's missions on their own.
- A runner opens the app, sees this week's missions and their rank, and earns rewards.
