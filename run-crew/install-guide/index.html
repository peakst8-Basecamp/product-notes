iPhone · via TestFlight

Five steps on iPhone

peakst8 is on iPhone through TestFlight, Apple's app for early releases. Join with our invite link and you're in.

  1. 01
    Install TestFlightSearch for TestFlight in the App Store and tap Get. It's Apple's own app, with the blue propeller icon. Download TestFlight
  2. 02
    Tap the invite linkOpen it on your iPhone. It takes you straight to peakst8 in TestFlight.
    testflight.apple.com/join/bJPU3a55
  3. 03
    Accept the invitationTestFlight opens peakst8. Tap Accept, then Install.
  4. 04
    Turn on updatesScroll down peakst8's page in TestFlight and switch these on:
    Automatic UpdatesPush NotificationsEmail Notifications
  5. 05
    Use it like any other appMove peakst8 to your home screen and open it from there.

Reading this on a laptop? Scan with your iPhone camera to open the invite link.
