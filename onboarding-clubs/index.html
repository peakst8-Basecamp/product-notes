4. Onboarding a club

A club counts as onboarded when it has at least one weekly programme live and its first paid bookings on peakst8.

1 · Shortlist and callPick the club

Shoumik picks a HYROX gym or run club and walks the founder through peakst8.

2 · Set upClub and sessions live

The club, its hosts and its weekly sessions go live. Until recurring sessions ship, each date is its own training event, named to join a programme later.

3 · First bookingsMembers book on peakst8

The club shares its programme link; the first sessions are booked and paid on peakst8.

4 · MembershipMonthly plan

Pilot clubs offer Gold, Silver and Bronze plans once memberships are live.

5 · Weekly check-inBookings, members, payout

Shoumik reviews the week with the club and fixes what got in the way.

First wave: 10 founding HYROX gyms (the memo's first 30 days) and two run clubs for the membership pilot.

5. What we build to make onboarding easy

A leaner cut of Shoumik's spec, plus the membership pilot.

Recurring sessions

  1. A club admin creates a training session and marks it as repeating weekly, on the days they pick, until an end date.
  2. peakst8 creates every date at once, up to about 12 weeks ahead. Each date keeps its own seats, price and ticket.
  3. Athletes see one card for the programme, open it, pick one or more dates and book each one through today's checkout.
  4. The admin edits price or details for all upcoming dates in one go, or cancels a single date. Existing bookings keep what they paid.

Membership pilot

  1. A club sets up Gold, Silver and Bronze plans.
  2. A member joins and pays by UPI AutoPay, with a reminder before every charge.
  3. The plan applies when they book a session, and they can see or cancel it under my plans.
  4. The admin sees who holds each plan.

Cut from the spec for now: daily and monthly schedules, the daily rolling-window job (dates are made up front), series coupons, programme reports, the gym map. Cut from memberships for now: perks, the brand wall, guest passes, discount codes.

Why they fit: the membership code counts one booking per session, which is exactly what recurring sessions create. Build sessions first.
